PHASE 17 — RATINGS, REVIEWS AND DISPUTES

Implement post-assignment feedback and dispute management.

RATINGS

Allow appropriate parties to rate each other only after an eligible completed interaction.

Prevent:
- duplicate rating
- rating unrelated users
- rating before completion
- rating manipulation through unauthorized API calls

REVIEWS

Support text review with validation and abuse controls.

DISPUTES

Create dispute entity with:
- initiator
- related agreement/assignment/transaction
- reason
- description
- evidence references
- status
- assigned staff
- resolution
- timestamps
- audit history

DISPUTE STATES

OPEN
UNDER_REVIEW
WAITING_FOR_INFORMATION
RESOLVED
REJECTED
ESCALATED
CLOSED

FINANCIAL DISPUTES

Do not alter ledger history to resolve a dispute.

Use compensating financial transactions where required.

EVIDENCE

Securely store evidence.

Do not make uploaded evidence public.

ADMIN

Authorized support staff can review and resolve.

FLUTTER

Implement dispute creation, evidence upload, status and resolution view.

TEST authorization and state transitions.
