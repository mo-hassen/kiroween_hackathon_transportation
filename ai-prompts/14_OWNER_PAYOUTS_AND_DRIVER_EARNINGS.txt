PHASE 14 — OWNER PAYOUTS AND DRIVER EARNINGS

Implement settlement and payout workflows using the financial ledger.

OWNER

Display:
- gross earnings
- platform commission
- deductions/fees
- net payable
- payout status
- payout history

DRIVER

Display:
- gross earnings
- applicable platform fees
- net earnings
- pending earnings
- paid earnings
- payout history

PAYOUT STATES

PENDING
PROCESSING
COMPLETED
FAILED
REVERSED
HELD

PAYOUT SAFETY

Never mark payout completed because the Flutter app says it succeeded.

Only trusted backend/provider confirmation can do so.

IDEMPOTENCY

Every payout operation must be protected against duplicate execution.

RECONCILIATION

Design a mechanism for comparing internal ledger records with provider records.

ADMIN

Finance staff can:
- view
- investigate
- hold where authorized
- retry failed payout
- reconcile

They must not rewrite historical ledger records.

Add tests for:
- duplicate payout
- concurrent payout
- failed payout
- reversal
- provider timeout
- retry
- authorization
