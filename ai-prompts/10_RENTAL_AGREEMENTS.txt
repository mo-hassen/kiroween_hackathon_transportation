PHASE 10 — RENTAL / VEHICLE ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENTS

Implement formal owner-driver agreements.

AGREEMENT DATA

Include:
- owner
- driver
- vehicle
- start date
- end date
- pricing/financial terms
- commission rules/version
- responsibilities
- status
- agreement version
- timestamps

AGREEMENT STATES

DRAFT
PENDING_ACCEPTANCE
ACCEPTED
ACTIVE
EXPIRED
TERMINATED
DISPUTED

Do not allow silent modification of accepted terms.

Use versioning.

ACCEPTANCE

Record:
- actor
- timestamp
- agreement version
- accepted terms reference
- audit event

ASSIGNMENT

An accepted agreement creates or enables a valid vehicle assignment according to the approved business rules.

CONCURRENCY

One vehicle cannot have overlapping active assignments.

Use database constraints/transactions and appropriate application validation.

SECURITY

Only the involved parties and authorized staff can access agreement details.

FLUTTER

Implement:
- agreement review
- terms
- acceptance
- rejection
- active agreement
- history

BACKEND

Implement domain rules, transactions, audit and tests.
