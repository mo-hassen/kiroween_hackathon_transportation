PHASE 8 — VEHICLE MARKETPLACE

Implement the driver-facing vehicle marketplace.

Only display vehicles that satisfy backend eligibility rules:

- verified owner
- verified vehicle
- valid required documents
- active
- available
- not assigned
- owner not suspended
- vehicle not suspended

- browse
- search
- filters
- sorting
- vehicle details
- terms
- availability
- estimated cost/earnings information where approved
- owner information appropriate for privacy
- request/apply

OWNER EXPERIENCE

- terms

- accept/reject

BACKEND AUTHORITY

The backend must enforce every marketplace rule.

Use pagination.
