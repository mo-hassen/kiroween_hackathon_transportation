PHASE 7 — VEHICLE REGISTRATION AND VERIFICATION

Implement vehicle management and verification.

VEHICLE DATA

Support appropriate fields for:
- make
- model
- year
- vehicle type
- registration information
- plate information
- ownership information
- availability
- status

VEHICLE DOCUMENTS

Support:
- registration documents
- ownership evidence
- required insurance/compliance documents where applicable
- vehicle photos
- expiration dates

Do not assume a document proves ownership without verification.

VEHICLE STATES

DRAFT
SUBMITTED
UNDER_REVIEW
APPROVED
REJECTED
EXPIRED
SUSPENDED
ARCHIVED

VERIFICATION

Only authorized verification staff can approve vehicles.

A vehicle must satisfy all required eligibility rules before appearing in the marketplace.

EXPIRATION

Expired required documents must automatically affect eligibility.

SECURITY

Owner A must never access Owner B's vehicle documents through guessed IDs.

Use resource-level authorization.

FLUTTER

Build:
- add vehicle
- edit draft
- upload documents
- upload photos
- submit
- verification status
- rejection feedback
- expiration warnings

BACKEND

Implement validation, storage access, verification workflow, audit events and tests.
