PHASE 6 — VEHICLE OWNER VERIFICATION

Implement owner onboarding and verification.

OWNER PROFILE

Support:
- identity status
- owner type where applicable
- contact information
- required documents
- account state

OWNER VERIFICATION

States:
UNVERIFIED
PENDING
VERIFIED
REJECTED
SUSPENDED
EXPIRED
REVERIFICATION_REQUIRED

Only verified owners can submit vehicles for production marketplace use.

OWNER DOCUMENTS

Use secure private storage and controlled access.

Do not expose raw documents publicly.

AUDIT

Track:
- submission
- approval
- rejection
- suspension
- expiration
- reviewer
- timestamps

SECURITY

A normal owner cannot:
- approve himself
- approve his own vehicle
- modify reviewer decisions
- access another owner's documents

FLUTTER

Implement owner onboarding, verification status and required-document UI.
