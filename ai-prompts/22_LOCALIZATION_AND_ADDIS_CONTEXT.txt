PHASE 22 — ADDIS ABABA UX AND LOCALIZATION REVIEW

Review the application specifically for Addis Ababa.

LANGUAGES

Ensure the architecture supports:
- English
- Amharic

Do not hard-code user-facing strings.

MONEY

Use Ethiopian Birr formatting.

PHONE

Handle Ethiopian phone-number formats consistently.

LOCATION

Consider:
- neighborhoods
- sub-cities
- landmarks
- GPS accuracy
- permission denial
- unavailable location
- map failure
- local addressing realities

CONNECTIVITY

Design for variable mobile connectivity and low bandwidth.

UX

Review:
- Android-first usability
- touch targets
- readable typography
- accessibility
- loading states
- error messages
- offline/read-only behavior

CULTURAL/LOCAL FIT

Do not invent local legal requirements.

Do not use Western assumptions where they conflict with the product's actual Ethiopian operating context.

LEGAL/REGULATORY

Flag areas requiring professional/legal/regulatory review rather than making unsupported claims.

Review all visible text and flows.

Implement concrete improvements where appropriate and test them.
